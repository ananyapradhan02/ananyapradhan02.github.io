# Ananya Pradhan

I work at the intersection of technology and narrative — a computer science degree, an editorial training, and a career spent turning complex AI products into stories that move markets.

Currently I lead growth at an agentic AI startup, where I built the growth, product marketing, and GTM functions from scratch. Before that: four years at ServiceNow as a Senior Global Product Marketing Manager on a $1B+ AI platform portfolio, where I built a podcast to 2,000+ executive listeners and a vertical newsletter to 2,500+ subscribers across 20 countries — both from zero. Earlier still: growth marketing at HighRadius, and an editor's desk at Penguin Random House, which is where I learned the standard I still write to.

I maintain the [AI Pricing Observatory](/research), an open dataset of what AI actually costs, and write essays about growth, taste, and what AI does to both.
