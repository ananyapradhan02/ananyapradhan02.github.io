# The AI Pricing Observatory

My open-source research on longitudinal panel data for AI pricing — the open record of what AI actually costs.

Top AI companies changed pricing roughly 3.6 times each last year, and nobody was keeping the log. The Observatory keeps it: **25 companies, 94 SKUs, 25 documented pricing changes** — every meter, credit system, quota definition, and change event, source-cited and normalized to canonical units so anyone can compare vendors or test hypotheses.

**What it records:** pricing models (seat, usage, credit, outcome, hybrid) · plans with quota and "unlimited" semantics · usage meters with normalized rates · credit dictionaries, including whether failed agent actions consume credits · pricing change events with notice periods, grandfathering terms, and documented user reaction · bill-shock protections.

**Where it lives:**

- [GitHub — canonical source](https://github.com/ananyapradhan02/ai-pricing-observatory) (data CC-BY-4.0, code MIT)
- [Hugging Face Datasets — mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ananyapradhan/ai-pricing-observatory)
- Companion methods paper and quarterly *State of AI Pricing* report: forthcoming.
